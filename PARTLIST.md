## Panic button (battery-powered controller)

Parts for the handheld panic button that talks to `disco_server`'s `/api/panic` and `/api/boombox` endpoints over WiFi. Designed around deep sleep: the ESP32-C6 sleeps at ~15 µA and is woken by a GPIO edge from the panic/wake button; the OLED is fully powered off via a high-side P-channel MOSFET while asleep, not just blanked. Schematic in `Schematics/PanicButton/`.
